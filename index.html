01 / About us

Rooted in communities.
Focused on systems.

Community Pulse is a nonprofit organization registered in Zambia, established to contribute to healthier communities and more responsive health systems.

We believe meaningful progress depends on listening to communities, understanding the conditions that shape health, and using reliable information to improve decisions and services.

Our perspective brings together community participation, evidence, practical action and the wider systems that support health, including service delivery, information, resources, accountability and institutional learning.

“

Our purpose is to strengthen linkages between communities and the health systems that serve them.
